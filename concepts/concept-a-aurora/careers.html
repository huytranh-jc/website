Job Description

We're looking for a Unity Developer to join our core engineering team. You'll work on real-time mobile games from early prototype through live-ops, with a strong emphasis on performance, clean architecture, and shipping fast. You'll collaborate closely with designers and artists in a lean, high-ownership environment.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain gameplay systems, UI, and backend integrations in Unity (C#)
  • Optimize game performance for mobile targets (CPU, memory, battery)
  • Integrate analytics, ads, and live-ops SDKs with minimal footprint
  • Collaborate with designers to translate mechanics into shippable features
  • Contribute to internal tooling and code standards across projects

Requirements

  • 2+ years of Unity/C# development with at least one shipped mobile title
  • Solid grasp of mobile optimization: profiling, batching, memory management
  • Experience with common mobile SDKs (Firebase, AppsFlyer, ad networks)
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced, ownership-driven team

Nice to Have

  • Familiarity with AI code-assist workflows (Cursor, Claude Code, Copilot)
  • Experience with PC / cross-platform builds
  • Any live-ops or A/B testing experience

Benefits

  • Competitive salary, reviewed regularly — not locked to annual cycles
  • Flexible hours and remote-friendly setup
  • Work on titles with real distribution and player metrics from day one
  • Direct access to founders and zero bureaucracy
