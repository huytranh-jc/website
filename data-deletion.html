Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how to delete your account and data for games published by JoyCraft Games on Google Play and the Apple App Store:

  • Sand Scratch (Google Play and App Store)
  • Airport Jam - Passenger Sort (Google Play)
  • Ring Slide - Colorful Yarn (Google Play)

Delete Your Account in the Game (Sand Scratch)

Sand Scratch lets you delete your account directly in the app, on both Android and iOS:

  1. Open the game and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Read the warning and tap Delete to confirm.
  4. You need an internet connection. When the deletion finishes, the game shows "Account deleted"; close and reopen the app.

Deletion is permanent. Purchases are not refunded; non-consumable purchases (such as Remove Ads) can be restored from the store.

Request Deletion by Email

For any of our games (including those without an in-game delete option), email business@joycraftgames.net with the subject Account Deletion Request – <Game name> and include:

  • Game name and platform (Android / iOS)
  • Device model and approximate install date

We will reply to confirm and complete the request within 30 days.

What Is Deleted

  • Your cloud saved progress
  • Your game account (player ID)
  • Your player name and profile (including avatar)
  • The save data stored on your device (in-game deletion)
  • A request to our attribution partner (Adjust) to forget your device

What May Be Kept

  • Leaderboard scores, if a game has a leaderboard: they are not removed by the in-game deletion. Email us if you want them removed.
  • Purchase and transaction records held by Apple and Google, under their own policies.
  • Data held by third-party services used in the games (such as analytics and advertising). Each game's privacy policy lists these services and how to contact them:
    • Sand Scratch privacy policy
    • Airport Jam privacy policy
    • Ring Slide privacy policy

Retention

We complete deletion requests within 30 days of the request.
