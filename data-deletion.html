Data Deletion Request

This page applies to the Games app, developed by JoyCraft Games.

How to Request Data Deletion

Send an email to business@joycraftgames.net with:

  • Subject: Data Deletion Request – Your Game Name
  • Body: Your device ID or any identifier associated with your account (if applicable)

We will respond within 30 days.

What Data Can Be Deleted

  • Any personally identifiable information you have provided directly to us

Data Collected by Third-Party SDKs

The app uses third-party services (Firebase Analytics, Adjust, AppLovin MAX, Google Ads, Facebook Audience Network, Unity Ads) that collect data independently. To request deletion of data held by these services, please contact them directly via their respective privacy policies.

Data Retention

JoyCraft Games does not directly store any personally identifiable user data. All data is collected and managed by third-party SDKs (Firebase, Adjust, AppLovin MAX, etc.). Please contact each service directly to request deletion of your data.
