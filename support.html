Support & Contact

This page is for players of JoyCraft Games apps. If you have a question or run into a problem, we are here to help.

Contact Us

Send an email to business@joycraftgames.net and please include:

  • Game name
  • Platform (Android / iOS)
  • Device model
  • App version
  • A description of the issue and screenshots, if possible

Response Time

We aim to reply within 3 business days.

Purchases & Refunds

Purchases are processed by Google Play and the Apple App Store. Refund requests go through the store:

  • Google Play refunds
  • Apple App Store refunds

If you have trouble restoring purchases, email us with your order ID.
